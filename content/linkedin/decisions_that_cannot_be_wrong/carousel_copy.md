# Carousel: Decisions That Cannot Be Wrong

## Slide 1 - Cover

**The System That Knows When to Stop**

Building clinical AI for decisions where being almost right is not enough.

## Slide 2 - The Problem

**The weakness is not intelligence. It is authority.**

Modern models are excellent readers. They can search guidelines, algorithms, fact sheets, and test directories in seconds.

The risk is different: a fluent answer can look authoritative before it has earned the right to be trusted.

## Slide 3 - Hybrid Architecture

**Control the data boundary before the first prompt.**

The model never roams the data. It requests what it needs through a governed API layer, retrieval plan, and evidence bundle.

Trust begins when the organization controls what can be searched, cited, and exposed.

## Slide 4 - Agentic Farm

**Split intelligence into jobs you can audit.**

One agent understands intent. One retrieves. One packages evidence. One drafts. One critiques. One scores confidence. One renders the answer.

Small jobs create inspectable handoffs.

## Slide 5 - Debating Cycle

**Make uncertainty visible before it becomes risk.**

The critic checks stewardship, safety, evidence gaps, and missed alternatives. Consensus is deterministic. Confidence is rule-based.

When the answer falls below threshold, the system escalates instead of improvising.

## Slide 6 - Formatting Agent

**The right answer still needs the right shape.**

A diagnostic pathway should not read like a paragraph. A comparison should not hide inside prose.

The Formatting Agent turns clinical JSON into a deterministic UI schema: cards, tables, citations, warnings, pathways, and split-view source checks.

## Slide 7 - Copilot Scale

**From one test question to every ordering moment.**

Today: diagnostic test selection grounded in ARUP content.

Final phase: a provider copilot that reads lab history, encounter context, prior orders, and trusted guidance to draft cited care-plan building blocks.

That is a surface area measured in thousands of provider decisions per health system each month - and millions nationally.

## Slide 8 - Three Takeaways

**Three rules for AI that cannot bluff.**

1. Own the boundary, or the model owns you.
2. Design agents small enough to review.
3. Treat uncertainty as a workflow, not a weakness.

## Slide 9 - Close

**Zero ungrounded claims. Visible uncertainty. Human handoff.**

That is the bar for clinical AI.

This series will unpack the frameworks behind it: Hybrid Architecture, the Agentic Farm, the Debating Cycle, and the Formatting Agent.
